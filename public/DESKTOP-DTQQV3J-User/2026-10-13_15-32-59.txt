  進行比特幣（BTC）的量化交易策略回測，主要是透過歷史數據來驗證你的交易邏輯（如均線交叉、網格交易、指標突破等）在過去是否可行。   以下為標準、嚴謹的 BTC 回測流程，分為五個主要步驟與常見陷阱：第一步：明確定義交易規則（策略規則化）   將主觀的「盤感」或「看好」轉化為客觀、無模糊空間的程式化條件。進場條件（Long / Short）：例如「當 4 小時線的 RSI 低於 30，且突破 20 期均線時買入」。出場條件（Take Profit / Stop Loss）：例如「固定停損 2%、停利 5%，或當 MACD 出現死叉時出場」。資金管理：每筆交易所佔用的總資金比例（例如單筆不超過總資金的 2%）。第二步：選擇回測工具與歷史數據根據你的技術能力與需求選擇適合的平台：TradingView (Pine Script)：適合族群：初學者至中階交易員。優勢：介面直觀，可直接選取各大交易所的 BTC 永續合約（如 BTCUSDT.P）或現貨歷史數據進行視覺化回測。   Python (Backtrader, Pandas, VectorBT)：適合族群：具備程式基礎、需要高頻或複雜機器學習策略的量化交易員。   優勢：自由度極高，可串接 Binance、OKX 等交易所的歷史 Tick 級或 K 線數據。   第三步：設定回測參數（關鍵細節）   在工具中設定環境參數，務必貼近真實交易環境以避免失真：   手續費（Commission）：加密貨幣合約或現貨都有手續費，需設定正確的 Maker / Taker 費率（例如掛單 0.02%、吃單 0.05%）。   滑點（Slippage）：真實市場中，市價單成交價往往會比預期差一些。BTC 流動性雖好，仍建議設定 1~2 個 tick 的滑點。   回測週期（Timeframe）：如 15 分鐘線、1 小時線或日線。注意免費版工具的歷史數據根數限制。   第四步：執行並解讀回測結果跑完回測後，切勿只看「總報酬率」，必須透過以下核心指標綜合評估：最大回撤（Maximum Drawdown, MDD）：資金從最高點回落的最大跌幅。這決定了你能承受多大的心理壓力。夏普值 / 索提諾比率（Sharpe / Sortino Ratio）：衡量單位風險下的超額報酬，數值越高代表策略性價比越好。勝率與盈虧比（Win Rate & Profit Factor）：高勝率策略若搭配極大虧損（如勝率 90%，但一次虧光），依然會爆倉；理想狀態是勝率與盈虧比取得平衡。總交易次數：樣本數過少（例如三年內只觸發 5 次交易）的回測結果參考價值低。   第五步：樣本外測試與防範陷阱（重要）樣本內外切分（In-Sample / Out-of-Sample）：將歷史數據切成兩段。前段用來調整參數（優化策略），後段完全封閉不看，拿來驗證策略。如果在未見過的後段數據依然穩定，代表策略較具真實效用。   避開過度擬合（Overfitting）：不要為了讓歷史績效好看，無止境地新增條件去迎合過去的走勢，這往往導致「歷史表現完美、實盤一塌糊塗」。   注意未來函數（Look-ahead Bias）：編寫程式或指標時，確保沒有誤用「未來尚未發生」的數據（例如用收盤價計算卻在當根 K 線未收盤前就進場）。

  Backtesting a Bitcoin (BTC) trading strategy involves evaluating its viability against historical price and market data before risking actual capital. Because Bitcoin exhibits unique volatility, non-stop 24/7 trading, and variable liquidity, a structured backtesting process is essential.   Step 1: Strategy & Rule DefinitionBefore looking at historical charts, define every rule quantitatively. A rule set must eliminate guesswork.   Entry Logic: Define exact technical or quantitative triggers (e.g., "Buy BTC/USDT when 20-EMA crosses above 50-EMA on the 4H chart" or "Buy when RSI drops below 30").Exit Logic: Set predefined profit targets, trailing stops, or indicators for exiting (e.g., "Exit when RSI crosses above 70").   Risk & Position Sizing: Define account risk per trade (e.g., $1\text{–}2\%$ of balance) and stop-loss placement.   Market Type & Instrument: Clarify whether you are testing BTC Spot or BTC Futures/Perpetual Swaps.Step 2: Data Acquisition & PreparationThe reliability of a backtest depends directly on data quality.Select Timeframe & Range: Choose a sample that includes multiple market regimes—bull cycles, bear drawdowns, and sideways consolidation (e.g., 2020 to present).   Data Granularity:Daily/4-Hour Candles: Ideal for swing strategies.   1-Minute / Tick Data / Order Book Snapshots: Required for high-frequency or intra-day strategies to model realistic price paths.Data Cleaning: Handle missing candles, exchange downtime, and timestamp formatting across timezones.Step 3: Execution Modeling & Market RealitiesIgnoring realistic trading costs is the most common reason backtests fail in live trading. Incorporate the following parameters into your engine:Trading Fees: Include exchange maker/taker fees (typically $0.02\%\text{–}0.1\%$ per order).Slippage & Spreads: Model price impact, especially on higher-volume orders or during volatile market swings.Funding Rates (Perpetuals): If backtesting perpetual futures, factor in periodic funding rate payments (longs paying shorts or vice versa).Liquidation Mechanics: For leveraged positions, trigger liquidations using the exchange's Mark Price rather than the last traded price.Step 4: Run the BacktestSelect the method appropriate for your execution level:MethodBest ForTypical ToolsManual / Bar-ReplayInitial concept screening and simple discretionary rulesTradingView Bar Replay, Excel/Google SheetsNo-Code / ScriptingRapid rule testing and parameter optimizationPine Script (TradingView), StockHero, TrendSpiderProgrammatic EnginesHigh precision, complex logic, custom execution modelsPython (backtrader, vectorbt, ccxt), DolphinDBStep 5: Analyze Key Performance MetricsEvaluate performance beyond simple total net profit:   Profit Factor: Gross Profit ÷ Gross Loss ($>1.5$ is generally sought).Maximum Drawdown (MDD): The largest peak-to-trough decline in account balance (evaluates capital risk).Sharpe & Sortino Ratios: Risk-adjusted returns (Sortino measures downside volatility specifically).Win Rate & Risk/Reward Ratio (RRR): A high win rate with low RRR can be fragile compared to a low win rate with high RRR.Trade Count: Ensure a sufficient sample size ($\ge 100$ trades) to avoid statistical anomaly.Step 6: Avoid Common Pitfalls & Forward Test[!WARNING]Overfitting (Curve Fitting): Tuning indicators too tightly to match past price action causes the strategy to fail in real market conditions. Keep rules simple.   Out-of-Sample (OOS) Testing: Split data into an In-Sample set ($70\%$ for strategy development) and an Out-of-Sample set ($30\%$ for un-blinded validation).Paper Trading / Forward Testing: Deploy the strategy in a demo or simulated environment with real-time live data to confirm execution match.   Small-Scale Deployment: Begin live deployment with micro-position sizes before allocating full target risk.
